Why Campus Skill Exchange

A learning economy that runs on knowledge, not money

Most students already have something worth teaching — a language, an instrument, a programming language, a design tool — and something they wish someone would teach them. Campus Skill Exchange makes that trade simple: list what you offer, browse what you want, send a request, and meet up on campus or online.

  • Free for all verified university students
  • Request, accept, and message inside the platform
  • Build a portfolio of skills taught and learned
